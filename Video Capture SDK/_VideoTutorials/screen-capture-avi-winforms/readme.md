# Video Capture SDK .Net - screen-capture-avi-winforms (C#/WinForms)

The code of the [screen capture to AVI video tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9iZiSx7slI): a WinForms app with a live preview that records a whole monitor to AVI at the frame rate picked from a list, with or without the mouse cursor, the sound that plays on the speakers (or a microphone) as PCM or MP3 audio, and the video codec picked from the ones installed on the PC (MJPEG by default).

## Supported frameworks

* .Net 10
